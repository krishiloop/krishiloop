Docs · pitch and technical write-up

KrishiLoop

A plant doctor, farm adviser and weather warning on every farmer's phone, in their own language. Every answer comes with a receipt anyone can check, and if it turns out wrong, we recall it from every farmer who got it.

Docs v1.0updated 2026-11-20

Everything on this page is copied from the repository's own files. Numbers come with their source. Synthetic and public-dataset results are labelled as such.

1 · Problem

Nobody can check what an AI told a farmer

  • 608 million farms feed the world and 84% are under 2 hectares (FAO, 2019). Pests and pathogens take an estimated 30% of rice and 17% of potato yields (Savary et al., 2019).
  • AI farm advice is spreading, but nobody can check what an AI told a farmer, from which source, or take it back when it is wrong.
  • In our own test on synthetic questions, four open language models gave a dose nobody could check in 76% (85 of 112) of dose answers.
2 · Solution

A trust layer: advice with a receipt, and a recall

Plant doctor

Our own 11.5 MB model reads a leaf photo offline in the phone's browser (89 diseases) and shows why with Grad-CAM. When unsure, it hands the farmer to a person.

Farm adviser

This week's advice for the field, with the source, page and rule behind it. Answers are a sourced rule or a hand-off to the officer, never free AI text.

Signed receipt

Every recommendation is a signed W3C Verifiable Credential. Anyone can check it by QR or a short SMS code: Authentic, Recalled or Cannot verify.

Recall

If a rule or the photo model turns out wrong, we recall it and every receipt it touched turns Recalled.

Krishi officer

The nearest upazila agriculture office is always one tap away: all 544 upazilas, and the Krishi Call Centre 16123.

3 · Why now

AI advice is spreading faster than accountability

  • AI farm advice is spreading, yet advice that can be checked by a third party and recalled afterwards is rare: we found no example of advice that is signed, checkable and recallable (PORTAL_ANSWERS, problem statement).
  • Rule: EU AI Act Article 50 requires machine-readable marking of AI-generated content and applies from 2 August 2026 (European Commission; fact register GL-25, VERIFIED). It is an EU rule, not a global one.
  • Reach: 82.8 million people were online in Bangladesh at end-2025, 47.0% of the population (DataReportal, Digital 2026; fact register D2, VERIFIED).

8 · What is different

The advice itself is signed, checkable and recallable

  • Some advisers cite sources, but we found no example of advice that is signed, checkable by a third party, and recallable after the fact.
  • The advice comes from tested rule packs and a deterministic planner, not from a language model. Every rule quotes its source document and page.
  • It abstains when data is missing or a question is unsafe, and the case goes to an officer's inbox.
  • Photo diagnosis and chatbots already exist (for example Plantix, xarvio, and a BRRI photo app). Our layer is the receipt and recall, not another chat.
  • The rice pack was added with data only; no file under services/ changed.

15 · Data layer

Sources, storage, privacy

  • Sources: rule packs (each rule with a verbatim source quote and page), the open APIs above, and public datasets for the photo model. Data keeps its own licences, listed in corpus/manifest.yaml and on /proof.
  • Storage: embedded files only. JSON trust stores, a Kuzu graph, a LanceDB table, and a DuckDB warehouse committed as Parquet and Delta Lake tables (versioned, with time travel). No database server to host.
  • Privacy: sample farms carry no personal data. Photos stay on the phone: the leaf model runs in the browser. Live events carry ids and codes only. Officer names and phones are shown only as the government publishes them.
  • Contracts: JSON Schemas for plans, receipts, credentials and inbox items are validated in CI.
16 · AI layer

AI that is checked, and never writes the advice

Photo model

11.5 MB int8 model, 89 classes. A conformal safety gate chooses: diagnose, ask for a clearer photo, or refer. An energy score flags unknown photos. Grad-CAM, attention map and lesion mask show why.

Ask router

LangGraph router with hybrid retrieval (BM25 + multilingual-e5) over sourced rules, plus a Graph RAG step: sibling rules that cite the same page. The answer is a rule's validated text, or an abstention.

Language models

Used for development-time evaluation and for ahead-of-time voice clips (Gemini). No language model writes the agronomy or the advice.

Photo model weights are research weights trained on PlantWild (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0), non-commercial. A commercial version would retrain on licence-checked data.

17 · Security

Signed, recallable, read-only

  • Receipts are signed W3C Verifiable Credentials (eddsa-jcs-2022). Verification shows Authentic, Recalled or Cannot verify, never "correct".
  • Recall uses a W3C Bitstring Status List. A recalled rule or photo-model version turns every receipt it touched into Recalled.
  • Recall needs an admin token: POST /api/admin/recall returns 403 without it. The issuer key and admin token come from the environment, never from the repo.
  • The five MCP servers are read-only. Our rules server kept its DNS-rebinding guard on when we called it through a temporary tunnel (docs/mcp-over-tunnel.md): without the Host rewrite it answered 421, as it should. The tunnel was closed afterwards.
Visibility

Who can see these docs, and when

The team sets visibility and the publishing window in docs.config.json next to this page. It is deployed with the site. The page reads it first: if visible is false, or the time is outside the window, it shows "Not available right now" instead of the docs. If the file cannot be loaded, the docs are shown (fail open) with a quiet note.

A signed-in admin editor for visibility needs our live API, which is not deployed publicly yet (planned). This is a convenience gate for a static page, not access control: the page's files stay publicly reachable.
