KrishiLoop

The look of the KrishiLoop site and farmer app: cream pages, forest green, Fraunces headlines and pastels taken from Bangladeshi fields. Every value here is copied from apps/web/src/styles/index.css, and every text colour pair passes WCAG AA in both themes.

Colour

Green leads, pastels share the work, never yellow.

Forest green marks the brand and the one primary action per screen. Six field pastels each belong to one part of the product, so no page is all green. Meaning colours always come with an icon and a word. Each swatch shows the Cream value, then the Forest (dark) value.

Components

Five pieces the product is built from.

Actions

Button

The forest pill (accent on accent-ink) is the one primary action on a screen; every other action is the quiet outline (rule-2 border) or a text link (accent-text).

  • At least 48px tall on farmer screens, with a radius-pill shape.
  • It presses to scale(.97) over 160 ms.
  • An icon goes before the word, never instead of it.
  • Bangla labels switch to Hind Siliguri and may wrap. They are never truncated.
  • In the Forest theme the pill becomes pale sage with forest text, so it stays the brightest thing on the screen.
  • Never use a second filled pill on the same screen. Never use yellow.
Trust

Stamp

Authentic Recalled Expired Can't verify Sample

A stamp shows what a signed advice receipt's check found: Authentic (go), Recalled (ember), Expired (wait) or Can't verify (stop, dashed).

  • Every stamp has an icon and an uppercase word, so it still reads in greyscale and for colour-blind farmers.
  • Recalled always means "don't follow this". Pair it with what to do now.
  • The dashed carbon Sample stamp marks example, saved or replay data. It sits on the thing it labels.
  • Wait is earth brown (wait), never yellow.
Trust

GateChip

Very sureRice blast. Act on the advice.
Not sureTwo or three likely causes. Take a second photo.
New or riskySent to your Krishi officer.

A GateChip shows the plant doctor's answer after the conformal gate: Very sure (gate-sure), Not sure (gate-unsure) or New or risky (gate-risky).

  • The fills are the same in both themes, with forest ink #14261c on top (AA on all three).
  • The chip always says what happens next: act, take another photo, or "sent to your Krishi officer".
  • Never show a single percentage as the answer. The gate's set and its action are the answer.

The receipt is the signed piece of advice, a W3C Verifiable Credential (eddsa-jcs-2022), drawn as paper. It always prints in light colours, even in the Forest theme.

  • It uses radius-doc corners, a thin paper rule, shadow-lift and a dashed line above the mono ids.
  • It shows, in order:
  • 1. its stamp;
  • 2. the advice in one plain sentence;
  • 3. the rule, the source quote, the data and the confidence;
  • 4. the id and the QR that anyone can scan with no login.
  • Sample farms carry the dashed Sample tag on the receipt itself.
  • A recalled receipt swaps its stamp for Recalled and gains a "What to do now" line. It is never silently deleted.
Rules

How it should feel.

One idea per section

A label, a big title, at most one line, then the thing itself: a receipt, a map, a measured number.

Plain words

"Spray only if the spots spread", not "apply fungicide upon lesion progression". Short sentences, in Bangla or English.

Honesty you can see

Sample, saved, replay and simulated data carry a tag on the thing itself. Every number sits next to its source.

Never colour alone

Every state has an icon and a word, so it reads in greyscale and for colour-blind farmers.

Motion that explains

Expo-out reveals, UI under 300 ms, no 3D or WebGL; reduced motion gets short fades only.

Made for the field

Readable at arm's length in sunlight, 48 px tap targets, buttons that wrap and never truncate.
