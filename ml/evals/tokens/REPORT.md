**What we did to save tokens** (each visible in the numbers above or in the repo):
- **Model routing:** mechanical work (listing files, lookups, link collection) goes to cheaper subagent models; design, safety logic and evaluation stay on the strongest model (the rule is in the owner's global instructions, applied in every session).
- **Context isolation:** wide searches and builds run in subagents, so only their conclusions enter the lead's context.
- **Prompt caching** of the long, stable prefix (instructions, memory, tool definitions): the cache-read share above.
- **Compaction:** long sessions are summarised instead of restarted.
- **Files, not chat:** contracts and briefs live in files that agents read, instead of being pasted into every prompt (`docs/prompt-library/`).

## 2. The product: tokens per farmer answer

KrishiLoop generates **0 tokens** per answer: plans come from the deterministic planner (services/engine/planner.py, ADR-1) and Ask answers are the validated text of a sourced rule picked by BM25 + a small multilingual encoder (multilingual-e5-small); no generative model runs in the product today.
